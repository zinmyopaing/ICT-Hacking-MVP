Problem and Solution

Studying alone should not be the only option.

The Problem

University students often find it difficult to locate classmates who are studying the same course, available at the same time, or interested in collaborative learning.

Our Solution

Smart Study Group Finder provides one place where students can search, create, view, and join study groups based on course, subject, and study needs.

Core Features

Everything needed to start studying together.

Search Groups

Find study groups by course, subject, study mode, and availability.

Create Groups

Create a study group with meeting details, member limits, and group rules.

Join Groups

Join available groups and become part of a collaborative learning community.

View Profile

View created groups, joined groups, and personal account information.

Target Users

Designed for university students who want better study support.

The platform is intended for students who want classmates to revise with, prepare assignments, practice difficult subjects, or build regular study routines.
