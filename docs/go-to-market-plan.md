
Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

Smart Study Group Finder

---

## Target Early Users

University students who are looking for study partners, want to create study groups, or join existing study groups for their courses.

---

## Main Value Proposition

Smart Study Group Finder helps students easily find, create, and join study groups to improve collaboration and academic success.

---

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
|----------|-------------|---------------|----------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Class Facebook/Telegram Group | University Students | Find study partners quickly | Click demo link | Demo views / Clicks | Students actively use class groups |
| Campus QR Poster | Students on campus | Join study groups easily | Scan QR Code | QR scans | Easy access around campus |
| Instagram/Facebook Story | Student Community | Study together and succeed | Visit landing page | Story views / Link clicks | Popular social media platform |
| Lecturer/Class Announcement | Students | Improve collaboration for coursework | Open prototype | Number of visits | Trusted communication channel |

---

## Launch Sequence

1. Prepare the landing page and prototype demo.
2. Share the prototype with classmates and lecturers.
3. Promote through Facebook, Telegram, and campus QR posters.
4. Collect user feedback using Google Forms or prototype testing.
5. Improve the prototype based on user feedback.

---

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Students may not know about the prototype. | Promote through multiple channels such as Facebook, Telegram, and QR posters. |
| Low number of users testing the system. | Invite classmates and lecturers to participate in prototype testing. |
| Users may experience bugs during testing. | Fix reported issues quickly and update the prototype regularly. |
| Users may not understand how to use the system. | Provide a simple landing page with clear instructions and screenshots. |
