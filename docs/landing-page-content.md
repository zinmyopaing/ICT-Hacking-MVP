
# Landing Page Content

## Project Title

Smart Study Group Finder

---

## Target User

University students who want to find, create, and join study groups based on their courses and subjects.

---

## Headline

Find the Right Study Group for Your Course.

---

## Subheadline

Smart Study Group Finder helps university students connect with classmates, create study groups, and collaborate effectively for better learning.

---

## Problem Statement

Many university students find it difficult to locate classmates studying the same subject or course. Communication is often scattered across different platforms, making it hard to organize study sessions and collaborate efficiently.

---

## Solution Description

Smart Study Group Finder allows students to search for study groups, create new groups, join existing groups, and manage their study activities in one platform.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Requirement ID | User Value | Prototype Screen / Module |
|----------|----------------|------------|---------------------------|
| User Registration & Login | FR-02, FR-03 | Students can securely access the system | Login / Register |
| Search Study Groups | FR-05 | Quickly find groups by course or subject | Find Groups |
| Create Study Group | FR-06 | Students can create their own study groups | Create Group |
| Join Study Group | FR-07 | Easily join available study groups | Find Groups |
| View User Profile | FR-12 | View personal information and joined groups | My Profile |

---

## Benefits

- Easily find classmates studying the same course.
- Save time searching for study partners.
- Improve collaboration and communication.
- Organize study sessions more effectively.
- Support better academic performance.

---

## Call-to-Action

- Try Demo
- Explore Study Groups
- Login
- Create a Study Group

---

## Responsible Data Message

Smart Study Group Finder only collects the information required for study group participation, such as name, email address, course, and study preferences.
Personal information is used only to support the prototype functionality. No unnecessary sensitive information is collected or shared.
