# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

**Smart Study Group Finder**

## 2. Target User / Customer

- **Primary user:** University students who want to find, create, join, and manage study groups.
- **Secondary user:** Students who lead or organize study groups.
- **Decision-maker or admin role, if any:** System administrator who manages users, study groups, and inappropriate content.

## 3. Situation / Context

A university student is preparing for an upcoming examination or assignment but does not have classmates to study with. The student may use social media chats or ask friends, but the available information is often unclear, outdated, or scattered across different platforms.

The student needs a simple way to find study groups based on course code, subject, meeting date, time, location, and available member spaces.

## 4. Pain Point

The user may experience the following problems:

- Difficulty finding students who study the same subject or course.
- Study group information is scattered across social media and messaging applications.
- Users cannot easily check meeting details or available spaces.
- Students may join unsuitable or already full groups.
- Group organizers find it difficult to manage group information and members.
- Users may waste time asking classmates individually.
- There is no central platform for viewing joined and created study groups.

## 5. Product Introduction

The **Smart Study Group Finder** is introduced through a landing page and web-based prototype.

The landing page explains the problem, target users, main features, and value of the system. The user can click the **Try Demo** or **Explore Study Groups** call-to-action button to open the prototype.

The system allows users to register, log in, search for study groups, view group details, join groups, create their own groups, edit or delete groups they created, leave joined groups, and view their profile and study-group activities.

## 6. Value Message

For **university students** who experience **difficulty finding suitable classmates and organized study groups**, our **Smart Study Group Finder platform** helps them **save time, connect with appropriate study partners, and manage their study activities** by providing **registration, login, group search, group details, join, create, edit, delete, and group-management workflows**.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

**Team member:**  
“Many university students want to study with classmates but do not know where to find suitable study groups. Information shared through social media is often incomplete, difficult to search, or outdated.”

**User:**  
“How does this system help students?”

**Team member:**  
“Our Smart Study Group Finder provides one central platform where students can search for groups by subject and course, view meeting details, check available member spaces, and join a suitable group.”

**User:**  
“Can students create their own groups?”

**Team member:**  
“Yes. After logging in, users can create a study group with a group name, course code, subject, study mode, skill level, meeting date, time, location, maximum members, description, and rules.”

**User:**  
“Can users manage groups after creating or joining them?”

**Team member:**  
“Yes. Group owners can edit or delete their own groups. Members can view their joined groups and leave a group when necessary. The profile page also displays joined and created group information.”

**Team member:**  
“You can try the demo now by registering, logging in, searching for a study group, viewing its details, and joining it.”

## 8. Expected User Action

After hearing or seeing the product, the user should:

- Open the landing page.
- Click **Try Demo** or **Explore Study Groups**.
- Register a new account.
- Log in to the system.
- Search for a suitable study group.
- View the group details.
- Join an available study group.
- Create a new study group.
- View joined groups and profile information.
- Give feedback about usability and missing features.
- Share the prototype with other university students.
