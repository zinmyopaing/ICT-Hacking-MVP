# Final Demo Script

## Demo Duration

Recommended duration: **5–7 minutes**

---

## 1. Opening

Good morning/afternoon everyone.

Today, we are presenting our project called **Smart Study Group Finder**.

Many university students have difficulty finding classmates to study with. Our system provides a simple platform where students can create, search, and join study groups based on courses and subjects.

Our target users are university students and administrators.

---

## 2. User Scenario

A student wants to prepare for an upcoming exam but cannot find classmates studying the same subject.

The student registers an account, logs into the system, searches for an existing study group, joins the group, or creates a new study group if none exists.

The administrator manages users and study groups to ensure the system remains organized.

---

---

## 4. Evidence and Validation

The prototype was tested by classmates.

Users successfully completed:

- Register an account
- Login
- Create a study group
- Search study groups
- Join a study group
- Edit profile
- View dashboard

Feedback showed that the system is easy to use and helps students find study partners more efficiently.

---

## 5. Closing

In conclusion, Smart Study Group Finder provides a practical solution for helping university students collaborate through study groups.

The prototype successfully demonstrates all core MVP features, including authentication, study group management, user profiles, notifications, dashboard statistics, and administrator functions.

In the future, we plan to integrate a real database, real-time chat, AI-based group recommendations, and a mobile application.
